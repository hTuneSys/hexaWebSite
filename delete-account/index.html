Delete Your hexaTune Account

You can delete your account directly in the app:

  1. Open the HexaTune app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap "Delete Account"

If you cannot access the app, send a request to: info@hexatune.com

We will delete your account and associated data within 7 days.

  • Device tokens
